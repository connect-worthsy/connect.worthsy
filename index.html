Shipping

How and when your order reaches you

Where we ship

We deliver to addresses across India. Please give a complete address and a working phone number, because couriers call before delivery.

Processing and delivery time

  • Orders are confirmed within 24 hours on working days, then placed with our fulfilment partner.
  • Delivery usually takes business days from confirmation. Remote pin codes can take longer.
  • Delivery dates are estimates, not guarantees. Courier delays and public holidays can add time.

Shipping charges

  • Free shipping on orders of or more (after discounts).
  • Below that, shipping is .
  • Cash on delivery adds a handling fee of . Paying online avoids it.

Tracking

Once your parcel ships, we email you the courier name and tracking number. You can also check status any time on the Track order page with your order ID and email.

Multiple items

Items in one order can come from different warehouses and arrive in separate parcels. You pay shipping once.

Failed delivery

If a courier cannot reach you after repeated attempts, the parcel returns to us. We will contact you to re-ship; the cost of re-shipping may be charged.

Returns & Refunds

If something isn’t right

What you can return

  • Items that arrive damaged, defective or not as described.
  • Items that are not the one you ordered.

Report the problem within days of delivery. Change-of-mind returns are not accepted.

How to start a return

  • Email with your order ID.
  • Attach clear photos of the item and the packaging. An unboxing video helps for damage claims.
  • We reply within 2 working days with a replacement, refund or pickup instructions.

Refunds

  • Approved refunds go to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
  • For cash on delivery orders, we refund by UPI or bank transfer. We will ask for the details.

Cancellations

You can cancel before your order ships by emailing us your order ID. After it ships it cannot be cancelled, but you can still return an eligible item once it arrives.

Not returnable

Opened personal-care or hygiene items, and items damaged by misuse.

FAQ

Quick answers

How long does delivery take?

Usually business days after we confirm your order. You can see an estimate for your pin code on any product.

Is cash on delivery available?

Yes. A handling fee of applies. Online payment has no extra fee.

Why does it take longer than other sites?

We do not hold stock ourselves. Your order is placed with a fulfilment partner and shipped from their warehouse, which keeps prices low but adds a few days.

How do I track my order?

Open Track order and enter your order ID and email. Courier details appear once it ships.

What if my item is damaged or wrong?

Email us within days of delivery with photos. We will replace or refund it. See Returns.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes, before it ships. Email with your order ID.

Is buying here different from the Amazon or Flipkart pages?

Yes. Store items are sold and shipped by WORTHSY. The platform pages are affiliate links that take you to the partner’s site, where you buy from them.
